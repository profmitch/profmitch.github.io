Recording Attendance in Canvas Roster

Students looking at the Canvas Attendance feature to see how their attendance is recorded might see something odd.

Students in the Tuesday lab section will see their attendance marked on Wednesday (the day after), and those students in the Thursday lab section will see their attendance on Friday (the day after)

The reason for this is because this course records attendance for the lecture and the laboratory session, which occur on the same day for a section.

The Canvas app does not provide the ability to record attendance for more than one session in a day. (Which really is an indication that Canvas targets the K-12 organizations which would likely only need to record attendance once a day.)

The use of a The Day After recording is a workaround. So if a student in the Tuesday lab section sees a red X on a Wednesday, this means the student's attendance was not registered for the lab.

Fixing the Record. It happens that students fail to register their attendance in lab, even though I mention usually multiple times in the lab for students to enter their Attendance Code to indicate their attendance. Students can correct the record by showing in some way they were in the lab and did work. But students should make the effort to register their attendance just the same.

Why Attendance is Recorded.

  • Community (two-year) colleges generally require instructors to record attendance of each lecture and lab session (if the course has a lab). They do this to comply with federal regulations on financial aid showing student participation and engagement. They also do it for the potential need to justify to politicians and others that community colleges are serving their purpose as an entry vehicle to post-secondary education. Even though no one may closely pay attention to the data today, it is always possible that in the future, there may be some trying find reasons to end the community college system and the absence of helpful data like attendance would serve that cause. And as chemists, we love to collect data, don't we?
  • Attendance is not part of the grading of the course. Only your scoring on exams and assignments are factors in the course grading.
