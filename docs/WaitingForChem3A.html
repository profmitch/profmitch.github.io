Want To Take Chemistry 3A?

So you want to take Chem 3A and the College is getting in the way of your ambitions?

I completely understand and sympathize.

Total enrollment for this course is 60 students, who are divided into two sections of 30 each. The limitation is because the labs you are required to take for the course are limited to 30 students in the room.

Make Sure You Attend The First Lecture. At the very least you need to attend the first day of the semester that we have lecture. During the taking of Attendance, you will register your Student ID and Name with me so that I know you are trying to get in the course. If you continue to come to lecture, follow this same process so I understand your continuing interest.

On The Waitlist

The last day the College permits for adding any course in a semester is typically the first Monday in the 4th week of the semester. That provides a lot of time for enrolled students to decide to drop the course for many reasons (including because they think the instructor sucks, although I'd like to think that is rare). And this would open an opportunity for the waitlisted.

Access To Content On Canvas. While you are officially on the waitlist, you have the ability to look at all the course content, including homework assignments and quizzes. The limitation is that this is a read-only state, so you cannot submit work at all.

No labs. Unfortunately only enrolled students can be in the lab room and do work, so even as a waitlisted student you do not have access to labs at all. The first set of labs are not important in terms of actual doing chemistry though, so it's not that much of a problem..

Congratulations! You Got Enrolled!. Now for the unpleasant part: not only do you have to keep pace with work currently assigned, but you have to catch up on past work. This is not much of problem since it's only about one, maybe two, possibly three weeks of work. Experience shows that hard-working students quickly catch up, and no-late-penalty due dates are adjusted to give you time to do the past assignments.

Not Even On The Waitlist

Probably 9 in 10 instructors will tell students not on the waitlist just to leave the room on the first day of the semester. They will not bother with students hoping to get in but who are really way outside looking in.

Fortunately for students trying to get into my course, that's not the case. In fact, in a recent past semester, I had two students who were not even on the waitlist, they were able to add the course near the deadline at the end of the third week of the semester, and they worked their a**** off to complete the past work they had to catch up on, and got A grades for the course. Yes, that could be you! Their work gave me hope for the future of humanity.

Unofficial Waitlist. Students in this situation where they are not even on the College's official waitlist get put on my unofficial waitlist. Read the section above for waitlisted students as it applies to students on the unofficial waitlist. A complication of your status is that while officially waitlisted students get read-only status to Canvas, that is no so for students in your situation. I have to talk to Canvas to see if I can add students to an Observer or Waitlist status. It seems this semester I am unable.

No Guarantee: Coming To Last Day To Add

I need to say this. I had more than a few students who tried to get into the course and they hung tough for the first three weeks, sitting in on lecture, their fingers crossed for hope. And still they ended up disappointed as we reached the last add date and no space had become available as no student had dropped.

So there is a risk that any time and effort you make to add the course may not be rewarded because space does not open up for you as no students drop.

You need to make this calculation. Most students who won't continue will drop by the end of the first week. Some will drop in the second week. By the time they enter the third week, they are unlikely to drop. Some students wait for the first midterm exam to see if they will drop, but our exam does not happen until the after the add date.

I think beyond the 2nd week, you should consider making sure you register early enough so that you get Chem 3A in the next semester.
