    • Basics of Photography. A century ago few people had a camera and knew how to use it. Do you know the terms aperture, f-stop, depth of focus, film speed, ISO number, shutter speed? Everyone now gets a camera that can take stills or video, and thinks the camera will fix everything for them in making sure the object (or subject) will be properly illuminated, without thinking about these basic principles of photography
      If you want to level up your mobile photography beyond simple point-and-shoot snaps, learning core principles like lighting, framing, and manual exposure adjustments makes a world of difference.
      • Top Reads
        • Android Smartphone Photography For Dummies. by Mark Hemmings: A practical, easy-to-read book that breaks down fundamentals like finding natural light, using gridlines, capturing action, and mastering mobile editing apps.
        • Smartphone Photography: An Introductory Guide by Sarah McClellan. A great online article that outlines foundational composition principles like leading lines, the rule of thirds, patterns, and negative space tailored specifically to phone hardware.
        • The Art Of iPhone Photography by Bob Weil & Nicki Fitz-Gerald: Perfect for inspiration, this collection breaks down how professional artists compose, light, and process high-end mobile shots step-by-step.
      • Top Watches
        • Complete Beginner Guide. A comprehensive YouTube walkthrough covering essential rules of composition, leveraging golden hour light, locking focus/exposure, and creating depth with smartphone camera lenses.
        • Phone Photography Tutorial with Mike Browne. A practical video lesson focusing on working within the physical limits of phone cameras—mastering perspective, negative space, and handling fixed wide-angle focal lengths.
