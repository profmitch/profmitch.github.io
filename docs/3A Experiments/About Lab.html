About Lab

Why do Lab Experiments?

Lab experience is an essential component of science courses. In lecture, we learn some principles about how things work. In lab, we get to see how those concepts we learned about connect to "the real world" (observations and data in the experiment). Actively engaging in lab experiments helps you in mastering the subject matter, develop practical skills that will help you in your future careers, develop teamwork skills, and - helps you practice scientific reasoning and critical thinking. The lab experiments we will be doing tie closely to concepts we cover in lecture.

Partners

The VERY FIRST thing you will learn is that you will have a partner you work with during lab ("Teams of Two").

There are reasons for this.

  • In most workplaces, a business or organizations will have projects, and these projects are governed by the principles of project management.
  • Projects are done by teams, and the members of the team have particular roles, each member of the team must pull a particular weight.
  • During a lab, there will be a division of labor in getting equipment from cabinets, or performing certain processes.
  • Both members of the team must have the knowledge of what to do and how to do something, even though one member of the team does the activity. One member of the team is there to prevent or catch the other member of the team from doing something accidentally, perhaps in making a miscalculation or in avoiding a missed measurement.
  • The team members support each other: that includes in understanding how to collect data or in calculating results
  • The team does not just interact with each other in the lab, but outside of it too. Whether by phone or email or other communication means, the team helps each other to make sure the finally submitted report is perfect, with no points lost at all.

Partner issues

  • It is hoped that the partners get along so well that they may even become lasting friends...friends beyond the semester. It happens now and then.
  • If there are issues of "compatibility" however, partners are free to change to other teams
  • If there is difficulty such that a request is made of the instructor, the instructor can try to work a pairing that improves things
  • At some point, I will attempt an anonymous survey to help assist me in creating compatible pairings. I am open to suggestions to using some kind of e-harmony-like tool that makes this possible

What about odd numbers?

Using MyOpenMath Tools for Your Chemistry 3A Lab Reports

Why MyOpenMath Is Used In This Course

One of the most important reasons for using the MyOpenMath Assessment Tools that are integrated into Canvas for your Chemistry 3A Lab reports is that the tools awesomely help you make a better lab report.

The form in the MyOpenMath component is used for you enter the values you obtained during the laboratory session and verify your entries as reasonable and to confirm your calculations.

The form is set up to mimic what your printed data and calculations in look at the start.

When you enter your measured quantities into the form, it will warn you if things do not look at expected. If a mass is supposed to have four digits after the decimal point, which is what the balance presents you with, and you do not enter the correct significant digits, it will warn you that something is not right (you get the red "X" instead of the green checkmark)

It checks your calculations as well, such as when you are supposed to do an operation (add, subtract, multiple, divide) and it expects you get the correct value.

Imagine you did not have the MyOpenMath tool. There would be no ability to check your data and calculations for sanity and correctness. Then you submit your report and the instructor can only deduct a significant amount of points from your submitted work.

MyOpenMath is your friend here.

How about purposely making errors in the MOM form?

Remember the discussion about the precision of the buret? Remmember it was said that the buret is precised to 0.01 mL and so the data is expected to be precise to that?

The MOM informs you that it expects your data to have a higher precision. In fact, a heavy penalty is imposed for failing to be careful to pay attention to that if you submit it to that level. So make the corrections and avoid the heavy penalty.

Use The Online Form In/During The Lab Session. Yes, get your laptop, tablet, or phone out and find the MOM tool and fill the values of the data and your calculations in during the lab. It helps you get things right in your handwritten lab report too, it stops you from making errors or recording data that would not make sense, like getting masses (in grams [g]) or volumes (in milliliters [mL]) that would be wrong. The MOM tool was made absolutely for having you get things right while you are in the middle of doing those things!

Retry Deductions

The MOM form is set to discourage students from doing nothing but repeated trial and error instead of thinking first about what to do and limiting the attempts. This is the reason for retry deductions: it's like a minor sting. In fact, if a question has four choices (a multiple choice question), it may only give the student two tries, making the assumption the student is not doing the reading or studying. When taking a midterm examination, the student will not multiple tries at getting the answer. So the plan is to study.

How Students Will Work In Lab

This laboratory creates Teams of Two for a couple of good reasons (there should only ever be one Team of Three if there is an odd number of students in a lab session):

  • Division of Labor in Laboratory Setup. When the team is getting an experiment ready with its equipment setup, it is sensible for students to divide this task where each gets certain items for the setup and bring them to the benchtop.
  • Each member of the team will also divide clean up at the end.
  • Calculations: for any part of the report that involves calculations, students can do these separately BUT IT IS NOT RECOMMENDED. See next paragraphs

WHEN STUDENTS DO TASKS TOGETHER
There is one kind of task where students do the task together and without exception!!
That is any task where the result of the task creates an entry into the DATA table in the lab report. That includes taking readings of mass on a balance, readings of volumes on a volumetric device (use of graduated cylinder, volumetric pipet, buret). The students must absolutely work together on this data reading/determining task. Every member of the team MUST agree on the result of that task. If there is any question whatsoever, immediately get the attention of the instructor to help in clarification.

Reasons:

  • These data go into your lab report. If only one student is doing this important task that gets recorded in the data table of the lab report and the student makes a mistake, that can affect the entire experiment for the team. So all/both students of the team are fully involved in this type of task in the laboratory session. The team has a higher likelihood of catching errors in these critical tasks!!
  • All students must learn the details of a data-gathering task. At the end of the semester, each student will take the Lab Practical, and they do this alone (not in teams), and if a student does not know how to properly get a mass or volume, it is disastrous!
  • Calculations: doing calculations do not involve reading instruments that get mass or volume or other properties of substance involved in the experiment. But calculations become parts of the lab report and are entries in the MOM tool. Students SHOULD work together to check each other's calculations. Also, a calculation in Part 1 can affect how data is used in Part 2 of an experiment. So be coordinated on this.

Uneven Partnerships. Instructors will tell students to do calculations on their own because they want to avoid the situation where Partner 1 of the team is perhaps is a "take charge" type and Partner 2 of the team is pushed to the side, with the danger that Partner 2 is not learning what that partner should learn. This must never happen. Everyone must understand what is happening. It may be the case that Partner 1 seems to be running ahead of things, but in that case, Partner 1 has a reponsibility to make sure Partner 2 is keeping up. Call the instructor over to help every member of the team in keeping up!!

One Team of Three. At the start of any lab, if there is one Team of Three, that team will give their names to the instructor so that it is recorded. It makes it easier to track that group at lab report submission time.
